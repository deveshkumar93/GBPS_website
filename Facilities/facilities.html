OUR FACILITIES

Everything a
student needs.

At GYAN BHARTI PUBLIC SCHOOL, we provide modern facilities that support learning, safety and overall development — all within a calm, green and secure campus.

From smart classrooms to a well-managed mess and a large playground, our facilities are maintained to give students the best environment to study and grow.

AND MUCH MORE

More reasons to feel at home.

  • Well-stocked library with books for all classes
  • Computer lab with internet facility
  • Clean drinking water (RO) for all students
  • Separate washrooms for boys and girls, cleaned regularly
  • First-aid room with basic medical care
  • Transport facility with trained drivers and attendants
  • Hostel facility with safe residential support
  • Safe entry/exit points with guards at gates
  • Regular cleanliness and sanitation drives
  • Parking area for staff and visitors
